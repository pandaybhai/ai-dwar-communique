# AiDwar: WhatsApp Growth Engine

Build a marketing website for AiDwar — an AI-powered WhatsApp marketing platform for growing businesses, built on the official WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API). Three pages: Home (/), Privacy Policy (/privacy), Terms of Service (/terms).

Design: clean modern B2B SaaS. White background, dark text, emerald green (
#10B981) as the accent color, generous spacing, fully mobile responsive. Wordmark "AiDwar" as text in the navbar (no logo image). Do NOT use the WhatsApp logo or name in any logo/branding imagery — the word WhatsApp may only appear in descriptive text.

Home page:

Hero: headline "Turn WhatsApp into your #1 revenue channel", subheadline "AiDwar is the AI-powered marketing platform for WhatsApp — broadcasts, automations, shared team inbox, and an AI agent that sells while you sleep. Built on the official WhatsApp Business API." Primary CTA button "Get Early Access" (opens a simple form: name, business name, email, phone — store submissions in a waitlist; if no backend is connected yet, stub it to be wired later).
Feature grid (6 cards): AI Campaign Creator, Smart Broadcasts & Scheduling, Shared Team Inbox, AI Sales Agent, Contact Segmentation, Campaign Analytics — each with 1–2 sentence descriptions.
"How it works" — 3 steps: Connect your WhatsApp Business account → Import contacts & build campaigns with AI → Convert conversations into customers.
Trust section: "Official WhatsApp Business Platform · Your data stays yours · Made in India".
Footer: links to Privacy Policy and Terms, contact email support@aidwar.in, and this line: "AiDwar is a product of Meezoy Ventures Private Limited, Hyderabad, India. WhatsApp is a trademark of Meta Platforms, Inc. AiDwar is an independent platform built on the WhatsApp Business Platform."

Privacy Policy page (effective date August 13, 2026, owner Meezoy Ventures Private Limited, contact support@aidwar.in): write a complete professional policy covering: what we collect (account details, business contact lists our customers upload, message content and delivery metadata processed through the WhatsApp Business Platform, usage analytics); how we use it (providing the service, AI-powered features, support, service improvement); that message data is processed via Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and subject to Meta's terms; that AI features may process customer data through third-party AI providers under strict confidentiality; data sharing limited to service providers (cloud hosting, Meta, AI providers); security measures; data retention; user rights (access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent); compliance with India's DPDP Act 2023; grievance contact; policy update process.

Terms of Service page (same date/entity): service description; account registration and responsibility; acceptable use — customers must obtain opt-in consent from their contacts, no spam or prohibited content, and must comply with the WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy and applicable law; we may suspend accounts violating these; customers are responsible for the accuracy and legality of contact data they upload; fees and billing (to be introduced; terms will be updated); limitation of liability; termination; governing law India, jurisdiction Hyderabad, Telangana.
