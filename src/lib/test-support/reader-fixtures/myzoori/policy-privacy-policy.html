PRIVACY POLICY

General Privacy Policy

The privacy policy for our website and native mobile applications is available here. Please note that myzoori.com reserves the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time without prior notice. We encourage you to review this policy periodically before continuing to use our website or mobile applications.

Personal Information We Collect

Protecting your privacy and securing your personal information are our highest priorities. We collect and use your personal information for various legitimate business purposes.

For example, we use the information you provide during registration on myzoori.com or our mobile application to process and deliver your orders. Your email address and phone number may be used to keep you informed about our latest products, services, offers, and promotional updates.

We may also use your date of birth, gender, contact history, purchase history, and payment information to provide personalised customer support and communicate with you through phone calls, SMS, WhatsApp, email, or social media.

By accessing or using our website or native mobile applications, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Privacy Policy and our Terms & Conditions. This Privacy Policy applies to all visitors, registered users, and customers using our website or mobile applications.

By providing your contact information, you agree to be contacted by myzoori.com regarding your enquiries, orders, offers, or services through phone calls, SMS, text messages, WhatsApp, or email.

Cookies

Cookies are small data files stored on your device by your web browser. They help us remember your preferences, improve your browsing experience, and provide consistent services.

myzoori.com does not store sensitive personal information in cookies for external purposes.

We use Google Analytics and similar technologies, including:

  • Google Display Network Impression Reporting
  • DoubleClick Campaign Manager Integration
  • Google Analytics Demographics and Interest Reporting

You may opt out of Google Analytics Display Advertising and customise your Google Display Network ad preferences through Google's Ad Settings.

We use both first-party cookies (such as Google Analytics cookies) and third-party cookies (such as DoubleClick cookies) to understand how visitors interact with our advertisements, website, and services.

Children's Privacy

Our website and mobile applications are not intended for children. myzoori.com does not knowingly collect, store, or sell personal information from individuals under the applicable legal age.

How We Use Your Information

All information collected through the website and mobile applications is owned by myzoori.com and may be used to:

  • Process and deliver your orders.
  • Respond to your enquiries and provide customer support.
  • Notify you about products, services, offers, and promotions.
  • Improve, maintain, and enhance our website and mobile applications.
  • Analyse website traffic and visitor demographics.
  • Measure user engagement and website performance.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

You may unsubscribe from our promotional communications at any time.

Sharing of Information

To fulfil your orders and provide our services, we may share necessary information with trusted third-party service providers, including:

  • Courier and logistics partners
  • Payment gateways and payment processors
  • Banks and financial institutions
  • Technology service providers
  • Marketing and communication partners

These third parties are authorised to use your information only as necessary to perform services on our behalf.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to unauthorised third parties.

Third-Party Services

Our website or mobile applications may contain links to third-party websites or services. Once you leave myzoori.com, the privacy practices of those third-party websites or applications will apply. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies before sharing your information.

Legal Disclosure

We may disclose your personal information if required to do so by law, court order, subpoena, governmental authority, or other legal process, or when we believe such disclosure is necessary to protect our legal rights or comply with applicable laws.

Aggregate Information

We may share aggregated and anonymised information, such as website usage statistics and demographic data, with business partners, advertisers, or other third parties. Such information does not identify individual users.

Marketing Communications

If you contact myzoori.com through phone, chat, email, or any other communication channel, we may use your contact details to send you updates about our products, services, promotions, and offers. You may opt out of receiving such communications at any time.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

myzoori.com reserves the right to update, modify, or replace this Privacy Policy at any time without prior notice. Continued use of our website or mobile applications after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our website or email us via the contact information available on myzoori.com.
