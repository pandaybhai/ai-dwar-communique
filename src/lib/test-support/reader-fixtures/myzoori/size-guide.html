SIZE GUIDE

Size Guide

Find the perfect fit for your jewellery. Use our size guide to choose the right size for rings, bracelets, and necklaces.

Measure the inside diameter of an existing ring or wrap a strip of paper around your finger and measure its circumference.

US Size Diameter (mm) Circumference (mm)
5 15.7 49.3
6 16.5 51.9
7 17.3 54.4
8 18.1 57.0

Measure your wrist using a flexible measuring tape and add 1–2 cm for a comfortable fit.

Wrist Size Recommended Bracelet
14–15 cm 16 cm
15–16 cm 17 cm
16–17 cm 18 cm
17–18 cm 19 cm

Length Style
16" Choker
18" Princess
20" Matinee
24" Opera

  • Measure your finger at the end of the day.
  • Avoid measuring cold fingers as they may be smaller.
  • If you're between sizes, choose the larger size.
  • For wide rings, consider ordering half a size up.
  • Contact our support team if you need sizing assistance.
