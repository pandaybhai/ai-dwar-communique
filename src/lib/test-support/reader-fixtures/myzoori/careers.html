JOIN ZOORI

We spent twenty years behind the factory floor before we ever stepped in front of it. We're not looking for people who just want a job in jewellery — we're looking for people who want to build the honest version of an industry that hasn't always earned that word.

WHY ZOORI

You won't be selling markup. You'll be building something we'd want to buy ourselves — real materials, real pricing, real craft. If that sounds like a smaller pitch than most jewellery brands make, it's because we don't need the bigger one.

OPEN ROLES

We’re always interested in meeting talented, motivated people who can bring fresh ideas and new skills to our team. If there are no current openings listed, you can still reach out and introduce yourself. We’ll keep your details in mind when a suitable opportunity becomes available.

HOW TO APPLY

Send your resume and a few lines about yourself to careers@myzoori.com. No formal cover letter is required—just tell us what you do, what interests you, and why you’d like to work with us.
